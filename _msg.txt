nucleo-f722: add dhry_216m and coremark_216m benchmarks; embed board photo

Port the disco-f769 benchmarks to the nucleo-f722 (same sources and
optimization flags, -Ofast -ffp-contract=fast -funroll-loops, no LTO -
GCC -flto hoists loop-invariant work out of the timed loop and would
inflate the score):

- dhry_216m: Dhrystone 2.1 @ 216 MHz, 12,000,000 runs.
  Measured: 972,841 Dhrystones/s = 2.563 DMIPS/MHz.
- coremark_216m: CoreMark 1.0 @ 216 MHz, 25,000 iterations.
  Measured: 924.35 iterations/s (27.05 s), "Correct operation validated."

Both verified on hardware over the USART3 console. The Dhrystone score is
much higher than disco-f769's 1.370 DMIPS/MHz because this board's malloc
heap is in the on-chip SRAM1 (the disco's is in the external SDRAM,
write-through); CoreMark is essentially unchanged (924 vs 932) since its
malloc'd list is a small part of the workload.

board_images/), record the measured results in both project READMEs and
the board/root project tables, and note the SFPU single-precision FPU
build in the benchmark notes. Also drop the accidentally committed
